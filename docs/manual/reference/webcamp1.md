# Webcamp1

This is the Webcamp1 users-defined programme. Webcamp1 is being held at rua lopes 77 2-D, lisboa during the 21st and 22nd of July, 2007. **Please**,feel free to add in new topics, or to add new items inside a topic.

## Suggestions for the programme

### Topics

#### RSS

- RSS tutorial clips
- How to use google reader?
- How to share interesting pages making a smart use of RSS technology?
- Maps and GeoRSS

#### Search

- Google custom search
- User-centred search tools, aggregators: digg, technorati, stumbleupon ...

#### Web2.0 tech

- Wikis --> How to use it in a collaborative way?
- Blogs --> tips for powerblogging
- Mashups!! What are they?
- Bookmarklets --> recipes ([ls](http://del.icio.us/griflet/system%3Ahas%3Ashortcut), goto, tr, mohid, goog, twit, tag, tagloo, tagcloud, subscribe, digg, tag).
- Screencasting!?! What is it? What tools? What potential?
- Offline / syncing
