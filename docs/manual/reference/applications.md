# Applications

Mohid Water Modelling System has been applied to several coastal and estuarine areas and it has shown its ability to simulate successfully very different spatial and temporal scales, below it can be found a list of the documented applications. The Mohid applications can also be explored through a [Google map](http://maps.google.com/maps/ms?msa=0&msid=100668346847265574861.00044592e345863240ab2&ie=UTF8&z=2).

## Estuaries

Including the main Portuguese estuaries

> -   **Douro** (Pina *et al.*, 2003; Silva *et al.*, 2004; Saraiva *et al.*, 2007b),
> -   **Guadiana** (Cunha *et al.*, 2000),
> -   **Lima** (Saraiva *et al.*, 2007b),
> -   **Minho** (Saraiva *et al.*, 2007b),
> -   **Mondego** (Saraiva et al., 2007b),
> -   **Tagus** (Portela, 1996; Pina *et al.*, 2000; Pina, 2001; Leitão, 2002; Braunschweig *et al.*, 2003; Pina *et al.*, 2003; Leitão *et al.*, 2003b; Fernandes, L., 2005; Saraiva *et al.*, 2005; Saraiva *et al.*, 2007b),
> -   **Sado** (Martins *et al.*, 2001; Martins *et al.*, 2002; Saraiva *et al.*, 2007b).

Along with most of the Galician Rias (Spain):

> -   **A Coruña** (Gomez-Gesteira *et al.*, 1999),
> -   **Ferrol** (deCastro *et al.*, 2002),
> -   **Pontevedra** (Taboada *et al.*, 2000; Ruiz-Villarreal *et al.*, 2000; Ruiz-Villarreal *et al.*, 2002; deCastro *et al.*, 2004),
> -   **Vigo** (Taboada *et al.*, 1998; Montero, 1999; Montero *et al.*, 1999; Gomez-Gesteira *et al.*, 1999).

And some northern European estuaries:

> -   **Western Scheldt (Belgium-The Netherlands)** (Cancino & Neves, 1999; Campuzano-Guillén, 2004),
> -   **Gironde (France)** (Cancino & Neves, 1999),
> -   **Carlingford (Ireland)** (Leitão, 1996).

## Coastal lagoons

> -   **Ria de Aveiro** (Trancoso *et al.*, 2005; Vaz *et al.*, 2005; Saraiva, 2005; Saraiva *et al.*, 2007b; Genio *et al.*, 2008),
> -   **Óbidos** (Silva & Leitão, 2005),
> -   **Ria Formosa** (Silva *et al.*, 2002; Martins *et al.*, 2004; Nobre *et al.*, 2005; Saraiva *et al.*, 2007b).
> -   **Patos Lagoon (Brazil)** (Janeiro *et al.*, 2008)

## Submarine canyons

> -   **Aviles (Spain)** (Villareal *et al.*, 2004),
> -   **Nazaré (Portugal)** (Garcia *et al.*, submitted).

## Coastal systems

> -   **Algarve coastal circulation (Portugal)** (Leitão *et al.*, 2005; Leitão, 2007),
> -   **Chilean Patagonian fjord region** (Marín & Campuzano, 2007),
> -   **Estoril Coast (Portugal)** (Fernandes, R., 2005),
> -   **Galician Coast (Spain)** (Leitão *et al.*, 2003a; Carracedo *et al.*, 2006),
> -   **São Paulo Province coastal area (Brazil)** (Leitão *et al.*, 2004).
