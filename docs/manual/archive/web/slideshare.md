## Wiki extension

Upload your slides at slideshare. They could be ppt, odt or pdf. To post them in this wiki simply

1. copy the wordpress line of code from your slide at [slideshare](http://www.slideshare.net),
2. extract and paste the code snippet in the wiki article as illustrated below,
3. that's it!

`<slides>id=1234&doc=myslide-4312</slides>`
