# Flickr

> **Archived page.** This content is kept for historical reference only. It may be out of date, incomplete or unrelated to the current MOHID documentation, and it is not maintained.

With Flickr, you can upload photos by email attachment. You can also mail 2 blog your photos to a pre-configured blog.

## Tag photos in email

```
Subject: Lucy, my new cat tags: cute "black cat"
```

```
Body: Lucy does the funniest dance moves!
Isn't she cute?
```

Or you could send them in the body:

```
Subject: Lucy, my new cat
```

```
Body: Lucy does the funniest dance moves!
Isn't she cute?
tags: cute "black cat"
```
