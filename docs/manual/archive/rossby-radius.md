# Rossby radius

> **Archived page.** This content is kept for historical reference only. It may be out of date, incomplete or unrelated to the current MOHID documentation, and it is not maintained.

The Rossby radius of deformation **a** is the horizontal length scale at which rotation effects (characterized herein by the frequency **f**) become as important as buoyancy effects (characterized here by the celerity **c** of the gravitational -or buoyant- restoring force).

$a = \frac{c}{f}$
